Your new website

It updates itself from your KSL listings, sends every customer message straight to your phone, and shows you how your cars are doing. You never have to touch it.

What we need from you (about 15 minutes total):
  • A link to one of your cars on KSL (so we can find your dealer account)
  • A Telegram "bot" that you create — 5 taps, instructions below
  • The phone number and email where customer messages should go
  • Your business details: name, address, hours, a short "about us", a logo if you have one

1. Your KSL account

The website reads the cars you have listed on KSL and shows them with photos, prices and details. When a car is gone from KSL, it disappears from the website within about two hours and is counted as sold.

  1. Open cars.ksl.com, log in, and open any one of your car listings.
  2. Copy the link from the top of the browser (it looks like cars.ksl.com/listing/12345678).
  3. Send us that link. That's it — we don't need your KSL password, and nothing changes on KSL.
Good to know: keep listing your cars on KSL exactly as you do today. Add a car there → it shows up on your website. Sell it and remove the listing → it's marked sold on the website. No extra work.

2. Create your Telegram bot (where messages arrive)

Telegram is a free messaging app. A "bot" is just a private chat that only you can see. When a customer fills in the contact form on your website, you get a message there within seconds — with the car's photo, the price, the customer's name, phone number and message. You then just call them.

  1. Install Telegram from the App Store / Google Play and sign in with your phone number.
  2. In the search box at the top, type BotFather and open the one with the blue check mark.
  3. Tap Start, then send the message /newbot.
  4. It asks for a name: type something like My Dealership Leads.
  5. It asks for a username: must end in "bot", e.g. mydealership_leads_bot. If it says taken, try another.
  6. BotFather replies with a long code that looks like 7123456789:AAH8f.... This is the token. Copy it.
  7. Send the token to us privately (text message or in person — not in a public post). It is like a password for the bot.
  8. Tap the link to your new bot in BotFather's message, press Start, and send it any message, like "hello". This lets the bot find you.

Want your staff to see leads too? Create a Telegram group, add the bot to it, and tell us the group name. We can route messages there instead.

4. What happens after that

  • Every 2 hours the website checks KSL and updates itself: new cars, price changes, sold cars.
  • Customer messages land in your Telegram bot chat with the car photo and the customer's phone number.
  • Every morning you get a short Telegram summary: visitors yesterday, most viewed cars, new messages, cars sold this month.
  • Dashboard: we'll give you a private link and a code. It shows views per car, where visitors come from, which cars sell fastest, and all leads.

Questions you might have

Do I need to update the website? No. Keep using KSL as usual.

A car shows as sold but it isn't (or the other way round)? Check the KSL listing is still active; if it is, text us and we fix it in a minute.

Can I change the look, photos or text? Yes — tell us what you want; design changes are quick.

What does it cost? Nothing to run. Only an optional custom web address (about $10/year).

Is my data safe? Customer messages go only to your Telegram (and your email if you want). The site never shows customer information publicly.
